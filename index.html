About

  • For: dentists and oral medicine/surgery clinicians, as a second opinion on peripheral (exophytic) soft-tissue lesions.
  • Not for: ruling out cancer, intraosseous lesions, or use without a clinician.
  • Evidence: validated at one centre (255 lesions, 21 malignant). External validation pending. Figures refer to the research model; this page runs the public model (about 1.6 points lower top-1 in the sensitivity analysis).
  • Privacy: everything runs in your browser; nothing you enter is sent anywhere or stored. Do not enter names or IDs anyway.
  • Same engine as the Python version: this page contains a JavaScript port of the Oranosis engine. It was tested against the Python reference on 45,900 inputs with 0 differences (see reports/js_equivalence.md in the repository).

Zeinali MR, Mirzaei H, Khalighi HR. Introducing Oranosis v1 (manuscript in preparation).
